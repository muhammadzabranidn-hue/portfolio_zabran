Hi! I'm Muhammad Zabran Assyafiq (Zabran), a Junior Web Developer and vocational high school student who bridges code with visual design. To me, web development goes beyond writing lines of code — it's about shaping living digital experiences.

My creative process remains calm, structured, and detail-oriented. Backed by a passion for UI/UX, photography, and digital art, I strive to build websites that are not only technically sound, but also tell compelling stories through clean, intentional design.

Right now, I'm learning, exploring, and building my creative journey step by step. To me, design and code isn't just about how things look, it's about how they feel, how they work, and how they connect with people.
